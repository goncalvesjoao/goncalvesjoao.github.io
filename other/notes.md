# Notes

Capture **problem → your responsibility → what you changed → what happened**. Distinguish what you built, led, helped with, or inherited. Use numbers only if you can support them; an observable qualitative result is valuable. Mark uncertain details for verification. Old tickets, pull requests, screenshots, release notes, and performance reviews can jog your memory without needing to disclose confidential material.
